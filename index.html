The five colors of Magic

Color lore →

White, blue, black, red, and green — the color pie that holds the Multiverse together. Original mana marks on this site, not the official pentagon.

White · Peace

Order, protection, and the open plains. White magic builds, heals, and holds the line.
Blue · Knowledge

Islands, libraries, and the long game. Blue magic draws, denies, and out-thinks.
Black · Ambition

Swamps and bargains. Black magic pays any price for power — including its own.
Red · Freedom

Mountains, impulse, and fire. Red magic acts now and lets the ashes sort themselves.
Green · Growth

Forests, instinct, and the wild. Green magic wants the world to become more of itself.
