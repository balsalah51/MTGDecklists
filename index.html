The color pie

Five colors. Infinite lists.

White seeks peace. Blue seeks knowledge. Black seeks power. Red seeks freedom. Green seeks growth. Every list on this site is one of those philosophies, shuffled and sleeved.

Original landscapes stand in for plains, islands, swamps, mountains, and forests — not official card art.

White

Peace, order, and the open plains.

Blue

Knowledge, patience, and the turning tide.

Black

Ambition, sacrifice, and the lantern in the fog.

Red

Freedom, impulse, and the mountain's fire.

Green

Growth, instinct, and the forest's strength.
