Modern

Non-rotating constructed from Eighth Edition and Modern Horizons sets. Regional Championships starting September 11, 2026 are Modern.

A twenty-year library of staples, still being rewritten.

Modern is the constructed format for the September–October 2026 Regional Championships that feed the first Pro Tour of 2027. Spotlight: The Hobbit in Dallas (Sept 4–6) was Modern.

Lists live on the format pages. Shop gear uses the same Amazon short links as One Piece Deck Base. Card buy buttons use the same TCGplayer partner ID.
