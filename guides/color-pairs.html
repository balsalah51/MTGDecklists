Color pairs

The ten two-color guilds plus shards and wedges. Format pages rank the combos that are actually posting.

Azorius
WU

Law and structure. Azorius lists want the game to stay on script — counters, flyers, and a tidy end step.

Dimir
UB

Secrecy and information. Dimir midrange mills, trades, and always has one more answer in hand.

Rakdos
BR

Pleasure and pain. Rakdos wants to attack, discard, and turn life totals into a countdown.

Gruul
RG

Instinct and riot. Gruul is stompy creatures, extra lands, and a race the control player did not schedule.

Selesnya
GW

Community and growth. Selesnya tokens, enchantments, and go-wide boards that look like a festival.

Orzhov
WB

Debt and devotion. Orzhov drains, taxes, and turns every trade into a better rate.

Izzet
UR

Genius and impulse. Izzet spells, prowess, and the joy of a stacked trigger on turn three.

Golgari
BG

Life and decay. Golgari midrange is the graveyard as a second hand.

Boros
RW

Justice at a sprint. Boros energy, equipment, and the red-white wish to win this combat.

Simic
GU

Adaptation. Simic grows, draws, and mutates the board until the math is unfair.

Esper
WUB

The shard of control: white's law, blue's answers, black's cost.

Grixis
UBR

The shard of ambition: card advantage with a cruel streak.

Jund
BRG

The shard of survival: midrange that eats whatever is across the table.

Naya
RGW

The shard of the wild: big creatures and honest combat.

Bant
GWU

The shard of the citadel: value, blink, and a board that never quite dies.

Abzan
WBG

Endurance. Abzan is attrition, greasefang engines, and outlasting the loud decks.

Jeskai
URW

Cunning. Jeskai lessons, tempo, and the spell that is also a creature.

Sultai
BGU

Cunning from the swamp: graveyards, value, and a long game.

Mardu
RWB

Speed and sacrifice. Mardu wants the first strike and the last drain.

Temur
GUR

Elemental tempo: card draw strapped to a large green threat.
