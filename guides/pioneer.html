Pioneer

Constructed from Return to Ravnica forward. Izzet spells and green Cub decks led Pioneer after the August 2026 bans.

Where the guilds still remember their first war.

Pioneer sits between Standard and Modern (Return to Ravnica forward). After the Cori-Steel Cutter ban, Izzet spells shells and green Badgermole Cub piles split the winner's metagame in August 2026. Lists here are public tables from June–September 2026.

Lists live on the format pages. Shop gear uses the same Amazon short links as One Piece Deck Base. Card buy buttons use the same TCGplayer partner ID.
