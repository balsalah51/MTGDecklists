Ads and ad networks

Google AdSense is live. Nitro, Media.net, Playwire, and TCG affiliates are the next networks this site can apply for.

What is live today

  • Google AdSense (publisher ca-pub-1074015774205047) — Auto ads on every page. No traffic minimum. Already in ads.txt.
  • Amazon Associates — Shop sleeves, dice, playmats, and boxes. Same short links as One Piece Deck Base.
  • TCGplayer partner — Every card and “Buy list” button. Partner c/7670706/1780961/21018.

Display networks this site can apply for

Do not paste a second display network over AdSense without that network’s approval. Most premium networks replace AdSense rather than stack with it.

Nitro (NitroPay)
Best TCG fit. Overwolf’s gaming network already serves Moxfield, Magicspoiler, and other tabletop fan sites. Apply when traffic is real; they handle gaming-safe ads and typically replace AdSense.
Apply →
Media.net
Yahoo/Bing contextual ads. No official traffic minimum. English-language, text-heavy pages. Can run as an AdSense alternative, not a stacked overlay.
Apply →
Playwire
Gaming and entertainment header bidding. Strong for fan sites once sessions are steady. Replaces AdSense rather than stacking on top of it.
Apply →
Ezoic
Header bidding + layout tests. Use when the site has steady sessions. Typically replaces AdSense rather than sitting beside it.
Apply →
Publift / Setupad
Managed header bidding once pageviews grow. Higher RPM than raw AdSense; application required.
Apply →

TCG affiliates (no display ads)

  • Card Kingdom affiliates — Paper singles. Complements TCGplayer.
  • CoolStuffInc — Paper singles and sealed product.
  • eBay Partner Network — Singles and collections.

After Nitro or Media.net approval, add their lines to ads.txt and a snippet in the site header. Until then, AdSense Auto ads plus the two affiliate programs are the stack.

Skip popunder / push networks (Monetag, Adsterra pop, HilltopAds) — they hurt a fan TCG site’s reputation and AdSense standing.

What can sit next to AdSense

Google allows non-Google ads and affiliate links on the same page, as long as the page is still mostly content. Amazon Associates and TCGplayer are already stacked that way.

Media.net can add separate display units next to Auto ads if placements do not overlap. Nitro, Ezoic, Playwire, and Publift replace Auto ads and keep Google demand inside their auction — do not paste a second display tag beside AdSense without that network’s approval.

Lists live on the format pages. Shop gear uses the same Amazon short links as One Piece Deck Base. Card buy buttons use the same TCGplayer partner ID.
