Methodology

How this site counts lists: public tables, date windows, Duel Commander vs EDH, and what a metagame share means here.

What we publish

This site republishes public constructed decklists for news and commentary. We do not run events and we do not claim official winner’s-metagame numbers unless a page cites Magic.gg Metagame Mentor.

  • Source. Magic Online Challenge, Challenge 32, and league tables hosted on MTGGoldfish, plus three Magic.gg Metagame Mentor consensus lists.
  • Date window. Constructed formats: June–September 2026. Commander: May–September 2026 Duel Commander leagues.
  • Cap. Up to 800 lists per constructed format and 828 Commander lists, spread across events so a single league cannot fill the sample.
  • Commander. Tables are Duel Commander (1v1). Color identity and 100-card singleton are the same rules as EDH; the lists themselves are the 1v1 dialect.

How share is counted

An archetype’s share on a format page is that name’s count divided by the number of lists on this site for that format. It is a sample of public tables, not a weighted winner’s metagame. Names follow the source (Goldfish’s deck title).

Card images identify lists and come from Scryfall. Banner art is original and is not official Magic card art.

Lists live on the format pages. Shop gear uses the same Amazon short links as One Piece Deck Base. Card buy buttons use the same TCGplayer partner ID.
