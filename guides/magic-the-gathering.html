Magic: The Gathering

The trading-card game of the Multiverse, published by Wizards of the Coast. This library tracks public constructed lists by format.

“There is no one Magic. There are as many Magics as there are tables.”

Magic: The Gathering is a trading-card game of five colors, a thousand worlds, and a handful of ways to play. Players are planeswalkers. Cards are spells, creatures, and lands. A game is a story you tell with sixty cards — or a hundred, if you sit down for Commander.

This fan site is a library of public constructed lists: Standard’s current novel, Modern’s long workshop, Pioneer’s guild echo, Commander’s legends, Legacy and Vintage’s eternal halls, and Pauper’s commons-only charm. We are not Wizards of the Coast. We just keep the shelves dusted.

Lists live on the format pages. Shop gear uses the same Amazon short links as One Piece Deck Base. Card buy buttons use the same TCGplayer partner ID.
