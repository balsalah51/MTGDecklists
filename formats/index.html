Formats

“Magic is many games wearing one back. Choose the world that fits your table.”

Commander, Standard, and Modern are the three most-played right now; Pioneer, Legacy, Vintage, and Pauper sit beside them. Open a format for its color pie, guilds, and lists.

The living story
Standard

Standard is Magic’s current novel — the sets still on shelves, rotating when the next world arrives. From Wilds of Eldraine through The Hobbit, the next tide is Nauctis: The Sunken Realm in early 2027.

“Every rotation is a new chapter of the Multiverse.”

Modern

Modern is the great workshop of the last two decades: Eighth Edition forward, Modern Horizons included. It is the constructed format for the September–October 2026 Regional Championships.

Eternal, with a graveyard
Legacy

Legacy is eternal constructed with a banned list instead of a museum of restrictions. The Fantasticar was banned on August 10, 2026 — one more name on a very old wall.

“Force of Will still answers the storm.”

The first library
Vintage

Vintage is Magic as it was dreamed in 1993: a restricted list, not a wide ban list. The Fantasticar was restricted on August 10, 2026. Museums that still duel.

“Power nine, handled with care.”

The people’s Magic
Pauper

Pauper is constructed using only cards printed at common. It is clever, loud, and welcoming — Magic without the rare tax. Wizards clarified Secret Lair Zeta commons in September 2026.

“Every common is a potential champion.”
