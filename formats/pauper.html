Pauper

The most democratic constructed format — commons only.

Pauper is constructed using only cards printed at common. It is clever, loud, and welcoming — Magic without the rare tax. Wizards clarified Secret Lair Zeta commons in September 2026.

Pauper is constructed using only cards printed at common. Wizards also clarified Secret Lair Zeta commons legality in September 2026.
