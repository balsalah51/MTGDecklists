Formats and the banlist

Format FAQ

What are the three most popular formats right now?

Commander, Standard, and Modern — those names sit on the second line of the site banner.

Where do the decklists come from?

Public Magic Online Challenge/League tables hosted on MTGGoldfish, plus official Magic.gg Metagame Mentor aggregates. Each list page links the source.

Do buy links use affiliates?

Yes. Shop gear uses the same Amazon Associates short links as One Piece Deck Base. Every card and “Buy list” button uses TCGplayer partner c/7670706/1780961/21018.
