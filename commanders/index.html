Is this EDH or Duel Commander?

Public Magic Online Duel Commander (1v1) league tables. Color identity and the 100-card singleton rule are the same as a four-player Commander night; the lists themselves are the 1v1 dialect.

How does a commander get a page?

Every unique legend in the June–September 2026 tables on this site gets a landing page. Open a name to see every list, then buy the pile on TCGplayer.
