Is this EDH or Duel Commander?

Public Magic Online Duel Commander (1v1) league tables plus Goldfish public Commander lists. Color identity and the 100-card singleton rule are the same as a four-player Commander night.

How does a commander get a page?

Every unique legend in the sample gets a landing page. This hub lists names with at least five lists, sorted by color. Type a name in the search box — Basim Ibn Ishaq is included.
